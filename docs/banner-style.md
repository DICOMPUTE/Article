# Banner house style

The one style every generated blog banner renders in — enforced by
`scripts/banner/generate.ts` (engine A: procedural, seeded, no API keys, no
network), not suggested. A banner either comes out of the generator looking
like this, or a human supplies a real image instead.

**Look:** editorial flat duotone illustration with a light, uniform film
grain — a magazine spot illustration or a risograph print. Never glossy,
never 3D, never photorealistic, never "AI art".

## Composition

- One clear focal motif, placed right of centre, inside the upper 60% of
  the frame. The left ~40% and the entire lower 40% stay calm: the hero
  overlays the post title in white there, and the lower band must measure
  at least 4.5:1 against `#fff` (checked on every generated file).
- Asymmetric layout, generous negative space, sparse secondary marks only.
- Must still read at 390 px (card) and 120 px (search thumbnail): one
  motif, no fine detail.

## Palette

Two-to-three brand colours plus one accent per category, all taken from
the RACK token set. Nothing outside this table enters a generated banner.

| Role | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Ink (deep ground) | `#060914` | darker than RACK `--color-bg`, so white title text clears 4.5:1 |
| Ink (mid) | `#111A33` | RACK `--color-bg-elevated` |
| Ink (soft) | `#243460` | hand-tuned ramp step |
| Paper (highlight) | `#F7F6F2` | RACK light paper |
| Accent — engineering | `#4DE7FF` | chart series 1 cyan |
| Accent — security | `#2CE0A7` | RACK success mint |
| Accent — product | `#F09249` | RACK warning amber |
| Accent — research | `#C084FC` | chart series 3 violet |
| Accent — company | `#E85C61` | RACK danger red |
| Accent — design | `#9DB9FF` | RACK-family `--color-m-code-accent` |
| Accent — (unknown category) | `#8AA8FF` | RACK `--color-accent-text` |

The duotone ramp is fixed (deep -> mid -> soft -> paper); the accent
replaces at most one shape layer. Film grain is uniform, about ±4%
luminance — the risograph texture that strips any gradient sheen.

## Forbidden

Baked into the generator's design (there is no glyph or photo code path in
it at all) and the reviewer's checklist:

- Any text, letters, numbers, logos or fake UI in the image.
- Humanoid robots, glowing brains, circuit faces, holograms, neon grids,
  floating padlocks or coins.
- Glossy 3D or plastic render look, lens flare, god-rays, hyper-detailed
  fantasy style.
- People, hands or faces.
- Symmetric "epic" centring; stock clichés (handshakes, server corridors).

## Reproducibility

Every banner is `sha256(slug + seed-suffix)`-seeded. `banner.generated.json`
next to it records the brief (matched motif and keywords, accent, seed) and
the checks (dimensions, byte size, lower-band contrast). To regenerate with
a new composition: `bun scripts/banner/generate.ts --slugs <slug>
--seed-suffix <anything>`. To replace a generated banner with a human
image, drop in a new `banner.webp` and delete `banner.generated.json` — the
generator will never touch it again.
